# Companion games — how the closest references play (2026-09-23)

The survey behind THE COMPANION GAME at the top of `KinJam.md`. Roguebook, Cobalt Core, Dicefolk,
Wildfrost, Across the Obelisk and Monster Rancher details were checked against the sources below;
the rest is from memory.

| Game | Your side | Deck and turns | Positioning | Enemies | When one of yours falls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Slay the Spire** (baseline) | One hero | One deck, 5 cards, 3 energy | None | Telegraphed intents | Run over |
| **Roguebook** | Two heroes, front and back | **One combined deck** of both heroes' cards | Front hero takes the hits; a back hero's Block or Charge card **swaps them** | Intents | Its cards become 0-cost "songs"; **5 songs revive it**. After battle: low HP, 2 Wounds in the deck. Both down = run over |
| **Cobalt Core** | A ship with 3 crew | **One deck; each crew member adds its own card set** (unpicked crew's cards never appear) | **Move the ship to dodge telegraphed shots** | Per-cannon intents | "Missing" status makes a crew member's cards unplayable |
| **Across the Obelisk** | 4 heroes | **Each hero has its own deck, energy and turn**, in speed order | Minor | Speed order, shown on a bar | That hero is out |
| **Monster Rancher Battle Card** | 3 monsters | **50 cards that are the monsters' techniques**; discard cards to make GUTS, which pays for others | None | A mirror team | Its cards stop working |
| **Pokemon** (main series) | 1 active, party of 6 | 4 moves per creature | **Switching costs your turn** | Catchable wild monsters; trainers | Fainted until a Pokemon Center. The Nuzlocke fan challenge = permadeath |
| **Pokemon TCG** | Active + 5 bench | One deck; **energy is attached to a specific monster** | Retreat costs energy | Another player | Opponent takes a prize |
| **Dicefolk** | 3 creatures in a rotating chain; only the front fights | Dice, not cards | **Rotating the chain is the core tactic**; abilities trigger on rotate | **You pick the enemy's actions from its dice too** | Recruited at statues, not caught |
| **Wildfrost** | Leader + companions on a grid | Companions are deployable cards | Rows, front line | Visible countdown timers | **Injured**: attack and HP halved next battle. Leader dies = run over |
| **Inscryption** | 4 lanes | A deck of creatures | Lanes | **Queues its next cards in a visible back row** | Gone |

## What each one answered

- **Combined deck vs a deck per companion** — combined (Roguebook, Cobalt Core, Monster Rancher).
  Per-companion decks and turns (Across the Obelisk) triple the hand, the energy and the turn length.
- **Dodging** — Cobalt Core: the telegraph is what makes moving a puzzle rather than a guess.
- **A fallen companion** — Roguebook's songs keep the deck busy and make a knockout something to
  play back from; Wildfrost's injury is the gentle version.
- **Catchable enemies** — an enemy and the same creature as a companion never share numbers (one
  runs intents, the other plays cards), so catching costs AUTHORING, not balance.
